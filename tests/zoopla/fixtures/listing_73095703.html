A well-proportioned one bedroom ground floor flat set within The Courtyard on Goswell Road, EC1V.

At around 637 sq ft, the space is noticeably larger than most one beds locally. The layout works well, with a proper separate kitchen and a living space that feels open rather than cramped.

Wooden flooring runs throughout, giving it a clean, consistent feel, and the bedroom is a comfortable size. The bathroom is a full three-piece suite with a bathtub, which isn’t always a given.

The development itself is secure, with entry access and the added benefit of underground parking — something that’s increasingly hard to come by in this part of London.

Location-wise, you’re right on Goswell Road, so everything is within easy reach, whether that’s local shops, transport links, or getting into the City.

A solid option for a first-time buyer or an investor looking for something practical in a well-connected spot.
